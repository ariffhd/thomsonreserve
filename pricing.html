Why Thomson Reserve May Exceed These Benchmarks
Thomson Reserve has attributes that Jadescape and AMO Residence lacked at launch: a three-developer consortium (scale and quality signal), the confirmed CRL interchange at Bright Hill from 2030, and the NSC expressway opening from 2027. These are structural valuation multipliers that are not yet priced into the D20 market.
Investment Horizon: Buying at Launch vs. Waiting
The gap between Thomson Reserve's estimated launch PSF (~$2,300) and its indicative breakeven (~$2,050) represents a developer margin of approximately 12%. Buyers who enter at launch pricing before the CRL opening in 2030 and NSC opening in 2027 are positioned to benefit from both infrastructure and market appreciation during the construction period.
Rental Yield Projection
Based on comparable Upper Thomson rental data (Jadescape 3BR: ~$5,500–$6,500/month; AMO Residence 3BR: ~$5,800–$6,800/month), a Thomson Reserve 3BR at ~$2.5M purchase price would yield approximately 2.6–3.1% gross rental yield at TOP — competitive for an RCR development with tri-line MRT access and 1km school proximity.

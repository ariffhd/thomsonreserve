What to Expect

Thomson Reserve Site Plan — What the 5-Hectare Scale Enables

Confirmed by the developer: Thomson Reserve is themed “Calmness Grounded in Nature,” with a nature-inspired masterplan across 6 towers, 80 lifestyle facilities and two distinct architectural collections. Based on the confirmed site area of approximately 540,000 sq ft and plot ratio of 2.8, here is what the masterplan delivers.

Six Towers, Two Collections
Thomson Reserve comprises 2 blocks of 30-storey Luxury Collection towers and 4 blocks of 21-storey Classic Collection towers — 1,268 units in total. The Luxury Collection is expected to enjoy fantastic views, including towards the SICC (Singapore Island Country Club) side.
80 Lifestyle Facilities
The masterplan delivers 80 lifestyle facilities under the theme “Calmness Grounded in Nature,” anchored by three signature clubs — the Grand Clubhouse, Island Club and Wellness Club — alongside pools, function rooms and nature-inspired landscaping.
Two Design Palettes
The Luxury Collection is finished in teal-blue “Morning Mist” tones, while the Classic Collection is finished in beige, muted “Stone Garden” tones — giving each collection a distinct architectural identity within the same estate.
Ground-Floor Privacy
Ground floor units are designed with a grass buffer separating the Private Enclosed Space (PES) from the pool — adding a layer of privacy and greenery between ground-level homes and communal facilities.
2 Entry/Exit Points & Sheltered MRT Access
The development has 2 entry/exit points, with fully sheltered access from the main gate all the way to Upper Thomson MRT (TE8) — a specification that materially affects liveability and rental demand, especially with the Bright Hill CRL interchange arriving in 2030.
1,014 Carpark Lots
Thomson Reserve provides 1,014 carpark lots — approximately 80% of total units — across basement car parking that preserves the ground-level landscape. This is a generous provision consistent with comparable UOL, SingLand and CapitaLand developments of this scale.

Which Thomson Reserve Stacks Will Be Most Valuable?

While the official site plan has not been released, buyers evaluating Thomson Reserve can use the site's known orientation and surroundings to anticipate which stacks will command premiums at launch. This analysis is based on the site's location along Bright Hill Drive and the surrounding land use.

Premium Facing Directions

North & West — Reservoir & Nature Views

Stacks facing north and west at Thomson Reserve will overlook Windsor Nature Park, the Central Catchment Nature Reserve and — for higher floors — MacRitchie Reservoir. These views are permanently unobstructable because the nature reserve is protected land. Historically, nature-facing stacks at comparable D20 developments (Jadescape, Sky Habitat) commanded a 5–10% facing premium at launch.

When the official Thomson Reserve site plan is released, buyers should cross-reference the stack orientation against Google Maps satellite view and URA Master Plan zoning of adjacent plots. Any plot zoned “residential low-density” or “nature reserve” to the north or west of your stack is a positive indicator for long-term view protection.

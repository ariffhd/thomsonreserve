MRT Connectivity

Upper Thomson TEL vs Marymount CCL — The Structural Difference

Thomson Reserve residents walk to Upper Thomson MRT (TEL), running directly to Orchard Road in ~14 minutes and Marina Bay in ~22 minutes with no transfer. The adjacent Bright Hill station becomes a TEL × Cross Island Line interchange in 2030 — adding direct access to Jurong Lake District, one-north and Changi Airport. Jadescape's Marymount MRT (CCL) is functional but has no equivalent forward connectivity catalyst.

Schools

Ai Tong School 1km Priority — A Decisive Differentiator

Thomson Reserve at Bright Hill Drive falls within 1km of Ai Tong School — one of Singapore's most competitive SAP primary schools. Jadescape at Shunfu Road does not. Thomson Reserve buyers gain Phase 2C(S) priority for Ai Tong School registration — decisive during oversubscribed ballots, and a consistent driver of family rental demand.

Investment Thesis

Which Delivers Better Returns — New Launch or Resale?

Jadescape launched at ~$1,700 psf in 2018 and now transacts at $2,100–$2,300 psf — ~30% appreciation in 7 years. A buyer entering today is entering near peak with no pending catalysts. Thomson Reserve enters with two structural catalysts: the Bright Hill CRL interchange (2030) and new-launch-to-TOP premium compression. The counter-argument: Jadescape buyers earn $3,500–$4,800/month rental income from day one, eliminating the 6-year construction wait entirely.

FAQ

Thomson Reserve vs Jadescape — Frequently Asked Questions

Is Thomson Reserve or Jadescape a better buy in 2026?

It depends on your timeline. Thomson Reserve suits long-term buyers, HDB upgraders and investors positioning for the CRL interchange. Jadescape suits buyers who need immediate occupancy or rental income with no construction wait.

What is the PSF difference between Thomson Reserve and Jadescape?

Thomson Reserve is estimated at $2,300–$2,600 psf (analyst projection, not yet released). Jadescape resale transacts at approximately $2,100–$2,300 psf. The new launch premium reflects developer margin and the forward CRL interchange catalyst.

Is Jadescape within 1km of Ai Tong School?

No. Jadescape at Shunfu Road is not within the 1km priority zone for Ai Tong School. Thomson Reserve at Bright Hill Drive is — a meaningful differentiator for families targeting this competitive SAP school.

Which has better MRT connectivity — Thomson Reserve or Jadescape?

Thomson Reserve has the forward connectivity advantage. Upper Thomson MRT (TEL) connects directly to Orchard and Marina Bay CBD, and Bright Hill becomes a CRL interchange in 2030. Jadescape's Marymount MRT (CCL) has no equivalent upcoming upgrade.

Can I buy Thomson Reserve if I still own a Jadescape unit?

Yes, but as a Singapore Citizen buying a second property you pay 20% ABSD. Decoupling strategies may allow one spouse to purchase Thomson Reserve as a first private property — consult a legal advisor.

How does Jadescape compare to Thomson Reserve in scale?

Jadescape has 1,206 units on a ~408,000 sq ft site (TOP 2022). Thomson Reserve is expected to have ~1,268 units on a larger 540,000 sq ft (5-hectare) site — both the two largest residential developments in District 20.

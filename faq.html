About Thomson Reserve

About Thomson Reserve

Q1.
What is Thomson Reserve?

Thomson Reserve is a forthcoming private residential development located at Bright Hill Drive in District 20, Upper Thomson, Singapore. It is the redevelopment of the former Thomson View en bloc site, jointly developed by UOL Group, Singapore Land Group (SingLand) and CapitaLand Development — a consortium that committed $810 million to secure the 5-hectare site through one of Singapore's largest approved residential en bloc transactions.

Q2.
Where is Thomson Reserve located?

Thomson Reserve is located at Bright Hill Drive, District 20, in the Upper Thomson area of Singapore. The site is within walking distance of Upper Thomson MRT (Thomson-East Coast Line), close to Thomson Plaza, and adjacent to green spaces including MacRitchie Reservoir and Windsor Nature Park.

Q3.
Who is developing Thomson Reserve?

Thomson Reserve is developed by a consortium of three SGX-listed developers: UOL Group Limited, Singapore Land Group (SingLand) and CapitaLand Development. This is the same consortium behind Pinetree Hill and Parktown Residence, the latter of which sold 87% of its units on launch weekend in February 2025.

Q4.
How many units will Thomson Reserve have?

Thomson Reserve is expected to comprise approximately 1,240 residential units across nineteen blocks, with unit types ranging from 1 to 5 bedrooms. This makes it one of the largest new launch developments in District 20.

Q5.
What was Thomson Reserve called before?

Thomson Reserve is the redevelopment of the former Thomson View Condominium, which was sold en bloc in 2024 for approximately $810 million. The site is sometimes referred to by competing sources as 'Thomson View Residences' or 'Thomson View Enbloc' — Thomson Reserve is the official name for the new development.

Q6.
What is the site area and plot ratio for Thomson Reserve?

The Thomson Reserve site spans approximately 504,314 sq ft (5 hectares) and is zoned for residential use with a plot ratio of 2.1.

The Thomson Reserve VVIP preview is estimated for Q3 2026. Registered buyers receive priority invitations to the showflat preview ahead of the general public, along with early access to floor plans, the site plan and indicative pricing.

Thomson Reserve's official Booking Day, when Options to Purchase (OTP) are exercised, is expected to follow the VVIP preview in Q3–Q4 2026. Exact dates will be confirmed by the developer closer to the preview opening.

Thomson Reserve's estimated Temporary Occupation Permit (TOP) is 2032, subject to construction progress and final regulatory approvals.

Q10.

The official site plan has not yet been released by the developer. Registered buyers will receive the site plan, along with stack-by-stack facing analysis, the moment it becomes available.

Pricing

Pricing

Q11.
What is the price of Thomson Reserve?

Official pricing for Thomson Reserve has not been released. Based on the land cost of approximately $1,178 psf ppr and District 20 comparable transactions, analyst projections estimate a launch PSF in the range of $2,300–$2,600. This is not an official developer figure.

Q12.
Why hasn't Thomson Reserve released its price list yet?

New launch developers in Singapore typically finalise pricing closer to the VVIP preview, after assessing market conditions, construction cost estimates and the response from early registrations. Thomson Reserve's price list is expected to be released alongside the Q3 2026 VVIP preview.

Q13.
Is Thomson Reserve expensive compared to other District 20 launches?

Thomson Reserve's estimated $2,300–$2,600 psf range is broadly in line with recent District 20 new launch comparables such as AMO Residence (~$2,100 psf at 2022 launch, now trading higher on resale) and Jadescape resale (~$2,100–$2,300 psf). Given the three-developer consortium and the upcoming Cross Island Line interchange at Bright Hill, the pricing reflects both quality and forward connectivity catalysts.

Location & Connectivity

Location & Connectivity

Q14.
Is Thomson Reserve near an MRT station?

Yes. Thomson Reserve is within walking distance of Upper Thomson MRT Station on the Thomson-East Coast Line (TEL), providing direct access to Orchard Road and Marina Bay without transfer. The adjacent Bright Hill station is confirmed to become a TEL × Cross Island Line interchange by 2030.

Q15.
Is Thomson Reserve close to MacRitchie Reservoir?

Yes. Thomson Reserve sits close to MacRitchie Reservoir, one of Singapore's most iconic nature reserves, offering residents access to scenic walking trails, jogging routes and outdoor recreation. The nearby Windsor Nature Park further enhances the greenery surrounding the development.

Q16.
Is Thomson Reserve within 1km of Ai Tong School?

Yes. Thomson Reserve at Bright Hill Drive is within the 1km priority registration zone for Ai Tong School, one of Singapore's most sought-after SAP primary schools. This gives Singapore Citizen families Phase 2C(S) priority during registration ballots.

Q17.
What other schools are near Thomson Reserve?

In addition to Ai Tong School (within 1km), Thomson Reserve is also close to Catholic High School and Raffles Institution (Bishan), both reachable within a short drive.

Buying & Eligibility

Buying & Eligibility

Q18.
Can HDB owners buy Thomson Reserve?

Yes. Singapore Citizens who own an HDB flat can purchase Thomson Reserve as their first private property and pay 0% ABSD, provided they sell their HDB flat within 6 months of Thomson Reserve's TOP or purchase completion, whichever is earlier. This is commonly referred to as the HDB upgrader pathway.

Q19.
Can foreigners buy Thomson Reserve?

Yes, foreigners are eligible to purchase Thomson Reserve, a private condominium, subject to Singapore's Additional Buyer's Stamp Duty (ABSD) rules. Foreign buyers currently pay 60% ABSD on residential property purchases.

Q20.
How do I register interest in Thomson Reserve?

You can register your interest directly on this website using the VVIP registration form. Registered buyers receive the official launch date, floor plans, site plan and price list directly from our sales team — before the public launch — along with priority access to the showflat preview.

Is there a showflat for Thomson Reserve?

The Thomson Reserve showflat is not located at the actual development site and is open strictly by appointment. Register your interest to receive the showflat address and book a viewing slot. Online registrants are entitled to Direct Developer Price with no commission payable.

Comparisons with Other D20 Launches

Comparisons with Other D20 Launches

Q22.
How does Thomson Reserve compare to Jadescape?

Thomson Reserve and Jadescape are the two largest residential developments in District 20. Thomson Reserve is a new launch (estimated TOP 2032) with an estimated $2,300–$2,600 psf, while Jadescape is a completed resale development (TOP 2022) trading at approximately $2,100–$2,300 psf. Thomson Reserve has the added advantage of the 2030 Cross Island Line interchange and Ai Tong School 1km priority, which Jadescape does not have.

Q23.
How does Thomson Reserve compare to AMO Residence?

Thomson Reserve and AMO Residence share the same lead developer, UOL Group. AMO Residence is a smaller, completed boutique development (372 units, TOP 2025) that sold 98% on launch day in 2022. Thomson Reserve is a much larger estate-scale development (~1,240 units) launching in 2026 with a more extensive facilities programme on a 5-hectare site.

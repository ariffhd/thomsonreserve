MacRitchie Reservoir Condo — Thomson Reserve at Bright Hill Drive

Thomson Reserve at Bright Hill Drive is Singapore's closest major new launch condominium to MacRitchie Reservoir. The development's western boundary adjoins Windsor Nature Park — which connects directly to the MacRitchie nature trail network — giving residents walkable access to Singapore's most beloved urban nature park.

MacRitchie Reservoir Park

Singapore's Premier Urban Nature Trail

MacRitchie Reservoir and its surrounding forest form the heart of Singapore's Central Catchment Nature Reserve — a 3,000-hectare ecological zone that is Singapore's largest protected area. Built in 1867, its surrounds have been a nature reserve ever since, making it one of the oldest protected habitats in Southeast Asia.

MacRitchie offers 10km+ of boardwalk and forest trails, the iconic 250m TreeTop Walk suspension bridge across the forest canopy, kayaking and canoeing, and abundant wildlife. It is Singapore's most visited nature area for morning exercise and weekend leisure.

Permanent View Premium

Why Nature-Facing Stacks at Thomson Reserve Are a Structural Scarcity

The Central Catchment Nature Reserve adjacent to Thomson Reserve is protected under the Parks and Trees Act and URA Master Plan. No residential, commercial or industrial development can take place within the nature reserve boundary — ever. This is an absolute, legally protected constraint.

In Singapore's property market where view corridors are routinely blocked within 5–10 years, this protection is exceptionally rare. Buyers of north and west-facing stacks at Thomson Reserve are purchasing a guaranteed, permanent view of protected rainforest.

At comparable Thomson corridor developments — Jadescape, The Botany, Lentor Modern — nature-facing units have consistently commanded 8–12% premiums over road-facing equivalents, and that premium widens over time as surrounding non-protected areas develop.

Nature Corridor

Thomson Reserve — Gateway to Singapore's Green Corridor

🌿
Windsor Nature Park
55 hectares of secondary rainforest adjacent to Bright Hill Drive. Home to the Sunda pangolin, flying lemur and 100+ bird species. Walkable from Thomson Reserve — no car needed.
🌳
Thomson Nature Park
A 2019 heritage park preserving a Hainan Village and ancient Tembusu trees. Connects to Windsor Nature Park as part of the Upper Thomson green corridor.
🚴
MacRitchie Trails
10km+ of forest boardwalk including the iconic 250m TreeTop Walk suspension bridge. Kayaking, wildlife watching — Singapore's most popular urban nature park.
🚢
Lower Peirce Reservoir
Built in 1912, surrounded by one of Singapore's oldest secondary forests. Peaceful boardwalk and bird-watching, 10 minutes from Thomson Reserve.
🌞
Bishan-AMK Park
62-hectare urban park with a naturalised river, cycling paths and event lawns — one of Singapore's largest community parks, 10 minutes drive from Bright Hill Drive.
📍
Central Catchment Reserve
Singapore's 3,000-hectare primary rainforest — permanently protected. Thomson Reserve's west-facing stacks overlook this protected zone forever.

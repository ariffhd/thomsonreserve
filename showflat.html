FAQ

Thomson Reserve Showflat — Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Thomson Reserve showflat located?
The Thomson Reserve showflat is located at Bright Hill Drive, Upper Thomson, District 20, Singapore. The exact address will be confirmed to registered visitors prior to the Early-Mid October 2026 preview opening. Register to receive the address and appointment confirmation.
When does the Thomson Reserve showflat open?
The Thomson Reserve showflat is expected to open for VVIP preview in Early-Mid October 2026. Registered buyers will receive priority access and invitations ahead of the general public. Book your appointment now to secure an early slot.
Do I need to pay commission to visit the Thomson Reserve showflat?
No. There is zero commission payable by buyers for any unit purchased at Thomson Reserve through this website. All registered visitors receive direct developer pricing.
What happens after I submit my appointment request?
Our sales team will contact you within one hour of submission to confirm your appointment date, time and the showflat address. You will also receive a confirmation via SMS and email.
Can I request a specific sales consultant?
Yes. If you have been referred by a specific PropNex agent or have an existing relationship with our team, please mention it in the message field when booking. We will do our best to accommodate your preference.

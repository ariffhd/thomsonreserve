Payment Scheme

Thomson Reserve — Normal Progressive Payment Scheme (NPS)

Thomson Reserve, as an uncompleted development, will follow Singapore's Normal Progressive Payment Scheme (NPS). Under NPS, payment is made in stages tied to the completion milestones of the building — meaning you only pay as construction progresses, not all at once at the point of purchase.

What NPS Means for Thomson Reserve Buyers

Cash Flow Benefits of Progressive Payment

Under NPS, Thomson Reserve buyers do not pay the full purchase price upfront. The staggered payment structure — tied to construction milestones — means your cash outflow is distributed over approximately 6–7 years from booking to legal completion. This gives buyers significant cash flow flexibility compared to buying a completed resale property.

For buyers financing with a bank loan, interest payments begin only on the amount disbursed at each milestone — not on the full loan amount from day one. This further reduces the effective carrying cost during the construction period.

Using CPF for Thomson Reserve

CPF OA for Progressive Payments

Singapore Citizens and PRs can use their CPF Ordinary Account (OA) savings to fund progressive payments for Thomson Reserve. CPF usage is applicable for BSD, the down payment portion, and subsequent progressive stage payments — subject to the Valuation Limit (VL) and Withdrawal Limit (WL) applicable to your property.

For 99-year leasehold properties like Thomson Reserve, CPF usage is subject to the property's remaining lease covering the youngest buyer to at least age 95. Register to speak with our sales team for a personalised CPF computation for your Thomson Reserve purchase.

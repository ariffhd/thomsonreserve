The Developer Consortium

UOL · SingLand · CapitaLand

Thomson Reserve is jointly developed by three of Singapore's most established and respected property developers — a consortium that collectively committed $810 million to deliver one of the most anticipated new launch condominiums in the Rest of Central Region.

The Consortium

Three Developers. One Landmark Project.

The decision by UOL Group, Singapore Land Group and CapitaLand Development to form a joint consortium for Thomson Reserve is itself a statement of conviction. Each developer brings a distinct capability — together they represent decades of experience, institutional financial strength, and a proven track record of delivering premium residential communities across Singapore.

The consortium develops Thomson Reserve through a dedicated joint venture entity, Tamarind Development Pte. Ltd. — jointly held by UOL Group Limited, Singapore Land Group and CapitaLand Development Pte. Ltd.

About UOL Group

UOL Group Limited is one of Singapore's most prominent property companies, with a diversified portfolio spanning residential, commercial, retail, hotel and serviced suite developments across Singapore and internationally. Listed on the Singapore Exchange since the 1960s, UOL has built a reputation for quality craftsmanship, thoughtful design and consistent delivery.

UOL's residential developments are known for their meticulous attention to layout efficiency, material quality and long-term capital value. Their projects consistently attract strong buyer demand — AMO Residence, launched in 2022 at Ang Mo Kio Avenue 1, was 98% sold on its launch day, one of the strongest performances recorded for a District 20 new launch.

In addition to AMO Residence, UOL's recent residential portfolio includes Pinetree Hill (District 21, joint venture with SingLand), The Watergardens at Canberra, and the award-winning Sky Vue. Across all projects, UOL maintains a consistent standard of finishing, communal facilities and landscape design that buyers have come to expect.

District 20 Track Record

UOL's most relevant precedent for Thomson Reserve is AMO Residence — a 372-unit development on Ang Mo Kio Avenue 1 that launched in July 2022 at an average price of approximately $2,100 psf. The project achieved 98% sales on launch day, reflecting the deep demand for quality UOL product in the RCR. This same developer conviction and execution quality will underpin Thomson Reserve.

About Singapore Land Group

Singapore Land Group (SingLand) is a leading property developer and investor in Singapore, operating under the same Wee family ownership as UOL Group. Their long-standing collaboration with UOL means Thomson Reserve benefits from a deeply aligned partnership — not two companies working across different priorities, but a single family of companies executing a shared vision.

SingLand's development portfolio is anchored by some of Singapore's most recognised commercial landmarks. Marina Bay Financial Centre, developed jointly with other partners, stands as one of Singapore's most prestigious business addresses. The Sail @ Marina Bay, one of Singapore's first integrated waterfront residential-commercial developments, demonstrated SingLand's ability to execute complex large-format projects with lasting appeal.

In the residential segment, SingLand has collaborated closely with UOL on multiple projects. Their most recent joint residential project, Pinetree Hill in District 21, launched in 2023 and has achieved strong sales performance — confirming the partnership's continued relevance to Singapore's discerning homebuyer base.

Why SingLand Matters for Thomson Reserve

Thomson Reserve is one of the largest residential undertakings in the RCR — approximately 1,268 units on 5 hectares. A project of this scale requires not just design capability, but construction oversight, financial management and project delivery expertise at an institutional level. SingLand brings exactly this infrastructure, ensuring that Thomson Reserve is delivered on time and to specification.

About CapitaLand Development

CapitaLand Development is the development arm of CapitaLand Group, one of Asia's largest diversified real estate companies with a presence spanning more than 40 countries. Their involvement in Thomson Reserve brings an additional layer of design ambition and sustainability thinking that elevates the project beyond a standard residential launch.

CapitaLand's most recognised residential projects in Singapore are notable precisely for their scale and innovation. d'Leedon — a 1,715-unit development in District 10 designed by Zaha Hadid — demonstrated CapitaLand's willingness to invest in world-class architectural ambition for their flagship developments. Sky Habitat in Bishan, designed by Moshe Safdie, set a new benchmark for large-scale residential architecture in Singapore's RCR. The Interlace, another Safdie-designed project in District 4, won the World Building of the Year award.

For Thomson Reserve, CapitaLand's contribution is expected to manifest in the masterplan's landscaping philosophy, facility design, and the sustainability features embedded across the development — all areas where CapitaLand has a demonstrated competitive advantage over developers focused purely on unit sales.

Design & Sustainability Philosophy

CapitaLand is one of Singapore's most sustainability-focused developers, having committed to net-zero carbon by 2050 and consistently achieving Green Mark certifications across their developments. Thomson Reserve buyers can expect green building features, energy-efficient systems, and landscape design that integrates naturally with the adjacent Central Catchment Nature Reserve — a rare and permanent natural asset on the doorstep of the development.

Why It Matters

What the Consortium Means for Buyers

Three SGX-listed developers committing $810 million to a single residential site is not a routine transaction. It is a considered, long-term bet on the value of District 20 — and it carries direct implications for buyers considering Thomson Reserve.

01
Financial Certainty
Three public-listed developers means audited financials, SGX disclosure obligations and institutional accountability. Thomson Reserve will be built. There is no developer risk — only construction timeline risk, which is standard for any new launch purchase.
02
Quality Assurance
UOL, SingLand and CapitaLand each have public reputations to protect. None of them can afford to cut corners on a flagship project of this scale and visibility. Buyers can expect construction quality, finishing standards and facility delivery that reflects their combined heritage.
03
Market Conviction
A land price of $1,178 psf ppr reflects deep conviction in District 20's trajectory. Developers at this level do not overpay for land — they price it on future demand models. Their willingness to pay this figure is itself a bullish signal for the Upper Thomson market.

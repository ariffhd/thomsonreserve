Project Core

Thomson Reserve — Development Overview & Project Factsheet

Thomson Reserve is a forthcoming private residential development at 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 & 11 Bright Hill Drive in District 20, Upper Thomson. The project is jointly developed by UOL Group Limited, Singapore Land Group (SingLand), and CapitaLand Development — through their joint venture, Tamarind Development Pte. Ltd. — a consortium that collectively committed $810 million to secure the 5-hectare site through Singapore's largest approved residential en bloc transaction since 2023.

The development comprises 1,268 units across 6 towers — 2 blocks of 30-storey Luxury Collection and 4 blocks of 21-storey Classic Collection — built around the theme “Calmness Grounded in Nature.” 80 lifestyle facilities are planned, anchored by the Grand Clubhouse, Island Club and Wellness Club.

The Developer Partnership

UOL, SingLand & CapitaLand

Thomson Reserve is the product of a uniquely powerful developer consortium. Each of the three co-developers brings a distinct and complementary strength to what will become one of the most significant residential launches in District 20's history.

UOL Group Limited
Over 50 years of residential, commercial and hospitality experience. District 20 track record includes AMO Residence (2022), 98% sold on launch day. Recent collaborations with SingLand include Pinetree Hill and V on Shenton.
Singapore Land Group
Wee family-controlled, with deep expertise in large-scale mixed-use developments. Portfolio anchors include Marina Bay Financial Centre and The Sail @ Marina Bay. Thomson Reserve is their most ambitious joint RCR undertaking.
CapitaLand Development
Institutional scale and global perspective. Notable for innovative master planning at scale — d'Leedon, Sky Habitat and The Interlace. Brings design sophistication to the 1,268-unit, 5-hectare masterplan.

Architectural Vision

Site Elevation — A 5-Hectare Masterplan

Thomson Reserve occupies one of the largest single residential plots available for private development in District 20 — approximately 540,000 sq ft (5 hectares). This scale enables meaningful separation between towers and the kind of landscaped communal facilities that define a true residential estate.

The site's elevated terrain along Bright Hill Drive contributes to a natural stack differentiation, with higher floors commanding unobstructed views across the Central Catchment Nature Reserve and MacRitchie Reservoir. Thomson Reserve comprises 6 towers — 2 blocks of 30 storeys (Luxury Collection) and 4 blocks of 21 storeys (Classic Collection) — with the Luxury Collection expected to enjoy fantastic views, including towards the SICC side.

North-facing and reservoir-facing stacks are expected to command a facing premium at launch — a pattern consistent with Jadescape (2018), the closest large-scale RCR precedent for this site.

Connectivity & Transit

Unmatched Connectivity at Bright Hill Drive

Thomson Reserve's most structurally compelling attribute — one that directly underpins both liveability and long-term investment value — is its access to three distinct MRT lines within a short walking radius. This is precisely why UOL, SingLand and CapitaLand were willing to pay $810 million for this site.

TEL · Thomson-East Coast Line
Doorstep to Upper Thomson MRT (TE8)
Upper Thomson MRT connects residents directly to the CBD (Marina Bay, ~22 mins), Orchard Road (~14 mins) and Gardens by the Bay — all without a transfer. Walk from Thomson Reserve estimated at 5–8 minutes.
Walk-to-MRT specification materially affects rental yield and resale capital appreciation.
CRL · Cross Island Line (2030)
The Bright Hill Interchange — 2030 Connectivity Multiplier
Bright Hill Station will become a full TEL × CRL interchange in 2030 — Singapore's eighth MRT line. Direct access to Ang Mo Kio, Jurong Lake District (second CBD) and Changi Airport without transfer.
Properties at upcoming MRT interchanges have historically appreciated ahead of opening. Buyers entering at 2026 launch pricing position for this structural premium.
NSC · North-South Corridor (2027)
North-South Corridor — Reducing CBD Drive Time
Singapore's longest transit priority corridor (21.5 km) opens from 2027, running Woodlands–CBD via Ang Mo Kio and Bishan. LTA estimates suggest ~15-minute reduction in peak-hour CBD drive time from Upper Thomson.
A structural valuation tailwind not yet fully priced into the District 20 market.

District 20 has long been one of Singapore's most sought-after family residential addresses. The Upper Thomson corridor combines the school network of the Bishan–Ang Mo Kio belt with the greenery of the Thomson Road corridor. Few RCR new launches in 2026 match this combination.

Within 1km of Ai Tong School

Priority Registration for Thomson Reserve Buyers

Ai Tong School is one of Singapore's most academically sought-after SAP (Special Assistance Plan) primary schools. The Thomson Reserve site at Bright Hill Drive places residents within the 1km priority registration zone — giving families Phase 2C(S) priority, the decisive factor for competitive school enrolment.

This is one of the most actively searched specifications among District 20 family buyers, and directly supports both purchase demand and long-term rental demand.

Background

The En Bloc Story — How Thomson Reserve Came to Be

Thomson Reserve stands on one of the most historically significant en bloc sites in Singapore. The former Thomson View Condominium — a 255-unit development completed in the 1980s — spent 17 years and five collective sale attempts before finally achieving a successful outcome in 2024.

The persistence of the Thomson View owners reflects the enduring desirability of the Bright Hill Drive location. The site's proximity to nature, schools and the upcoming Bright Hill MRT interchange made it a target for developers long before the CRL was approved. The final $810 million sale, representing $1,178 psf ppr, was the largest approved residential en bloc since Chuan Park's $890 million transaction in 2023 — a number that underscores developer conviction in the long-term value of District 20.

The High Court of Singapore formally granted the sale order on 1 July 2025. The acquisition was completed on 2 October 2025, clearing the site for what will become Thomson Reserve.

What to Look For

How to Evaluate the Thomson Reserve Floor Plans

Now that the official show unit floor plans are out, here is a framework for evaluating which layouts offer the best value and liveability.

Efficiency Ratio
Look for units where the liveable area (living room + bedrooms) is a high percentage of the total floor area. Corridor wastage and oversized balconies reduce efficiency. A good RCR unit achieves 75%+ efficiency ratio. UOL is known for squarish, regular layouts that maximise liveable space.
Dual-Aspect Units
Units with windows on two sides allow natural cross-ventilation, reducing air-conditioning dependency and electricity costs. For Thomson Reserve, north-facing windows capturing nature reserve views combined with east or south secondary exposure create the most desirable dual-aspect configurations.
Kitchen Configuration
Singapore families prioritise enclosed or semi-enclosed kitchen layouts for heavy cooking. Open-concept kitchens suit Western lifestyles. For Thomson Reserve buyers with cooking-intensive households, confirm the kitchen layout before committing — especially for 3BR and 4BR units.
Study Room Utility
Units labelled “+Study” or “+Flexi” add meaningful value only if the study is a separate enclosed room with a window. An alcove or open niche has limited utility as a dedicated workspace. Check dimensions — a proper study should accommodate at least a 120cm desk.
Balcony Proportion
Balconies are included in the strata area but excluded from GFA. For Thomson Reserve units with nature or reservoir-facing orientations, a generous balcony is a genuine lifestyle amenity. However, balcony area beyond 10% of strata area should prompt a review of the effective PSF you are paying for indoor space.
Yard & Utility for 3BR+
Three-bedroom and larger units at this price point should include a yard for laundry, a utility room or storeroom, and a helper's room in the 4BR configuration. These are baseline specifications for RCR developments priced above $2M. Absence of a yard in a 3BR is a flag worth querying.

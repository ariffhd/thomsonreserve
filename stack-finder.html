How This Works

Reading a Thomson Reserve Stack

Stack Numbers Are Fixed Per Tower
Each of the 55 stacks keeps the same unit type from its lowest to highest floor — e.g. stack 19 in Block 5 is always Type E1(L), the 5-Bedroom Suite, from level 3 to the penthouse. Only the view typically improves with floor level, not the layout.
Classic vs Luxury Collection
Blocks 1, 3, 9 & 11 (21-storey) form the Classic Collection. Blocks 5 & 7 (30-storey) are the Luxury Collection, with private lift access on every unit — the only two towers where this applies.
Facing Notes Are Our Analysis
The site-position note on each stack comes from our own reading of the released site plan (see the full Site Plan & tower breakdown), not from the developer. Confirm exact facing for your unit with the sales team.

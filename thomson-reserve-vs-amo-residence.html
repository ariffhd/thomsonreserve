MRT — Different Lines, Different Commutes

Upper Thomson TEL vs Ang Mo Kio NSL — Which Suits You?

AMO Residence sits adjacent to Ang Mo Kio MRT (North-South Line) — effectively a doorstep station providing direct access to Orchard, City Hall and Raffles Place. Thomson Reserve's Upper Thomson MRT (TEL) gives direct CBD access in ~14 minutes to Orchard and ~22 minutes to Marina Bay, plus the Bright Hill CRL interchange in 2030 for direct access to Jurong Lake District and Changi Airport — something Ang Mo Kio MRT cannot offer.

FAQ

Thomson Reserve vs AMO Residence — FAQ

Is Thomson Reserve or AMO Residence a better buy in 2026?

AMO suits buyers who need immediate occupancy or rental income and prefer a boutique community. Thomson Reserve suits long-term buyers wanting estate-scale facilities, progressive payment, CRL connectivity and nature reserve adjacency.

Did UOL develop both Thomson Reserve and AMO Residence?

Yes. UOL Group leads both. AMO was a solo UOL project; Thomson Reserve is a JV with Singapore Land Group and CapitaLand Development. Both share UOL's reputation for quality layouts and on-time delivery.

What is the PSF difference between Thomson Reserve and AMO Residence?

AMO currently trades resale at approximately $2,200–$2,400 psf. Thomson Reserve is estimated at $2,300–$2,600 psf (analyst projection). The gap is relatively narrow given AMO is completed and immediately occupiable.

Is AMO Residence within 1km of Ai Tong School?

Yes — both AMO Residence and Thomson Reserve are within the Ai Tong School 1km priority zone. School proximity is therefore not a differentiating factor between these two developments.

Can AMO Residence owners upgrade to Thomson Reserve?

Yes. AMO owners who sell before purchasing Thomson Reserve pay 0% ABSD (Singapore Citizens, first private property). Those who hold AMO and buy Thomson Reserve as a 2nd property pay 20% ABSD. Consult a legal advisor on decoupling strategies.

Firm Project Update — Unit Count, Masterplan & Timeline Confirmed

Thomson Reserve's project details have been firmed up. The total unit count has been updated from an estimated ~1,240 to a confirmed 1,268 units, spanning 2-Bedroom to 5-Bedroom types across 6 towers: 2 blocks of 30-storey Luxury Collection and 4 blocks of 21-storey Classic Collection. 2 & 3-Bedroom units make up 1,066 units (84%), while 4 & 5-Bedroom units make up the remaining 202 units (16%) — reflecting a deliberately limited large-unit supply. Only 3 unit types come with private lift access: 4-Bedroom Premium, 4-Bedroom Premium + Study, and 5-Bedroom Suite.

The development is themed “Calmness Grounded in Nature,” delivering 80 lifestyle facilities anchored by the Grand Clubhouse, Island Club and Wellness Club. The Luxury Collection is finished in teal-blue “Morning Mist” tones and the Classic Collection in beige “Stone Garden” tones. Ground floor units include a grass buffer between the PES and the pool for added privacy. The estate has 2 entry/exit points with fully sheltered access from the main gate to Upper Thomson MRT, and 1,014 carpark lots (~80% of total units).

The project is developed by Tamarind Development Pte. Ltd., a joint venture of UOL Group Limited, Singapore Land Group and CapitaLand Development Pte. Ltd., at 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 & 11 Bright Hill Drive. The consultant team is confirmed as: Architect — P&T Consultants; Landscape Architect — Eco Plan Asia Pte. Ltd.; Interior Design — 2nd Edition Pte. Ltd.; Main Contractor — Lian Beng Construction. Estimated preview is now Early to Mid October, with estimated launch Mid October (2026).

Project Officially Named Thomson Reserve

City Developments Limited officially named the development Thomson Reserve on 26 March 2026. The name reflects the project's position within Singapore's Upper Thomson nature and heritage corridor — adjacent to MacRitchie Reservoir, Windsor Nature Park and Thomson Nature Park.

The name was formerly referred to as the Thomson View en bloc redevelopment or the Bright Hill Drive GLS site. All official communications will now use Thomson Reserve as the project identity.

Site Acquisition Completed — Development Commences

The UOL · SingLand · CapitaLand consortium completed the formal acquisition of the Bright Hill Drive site on 2 October 2025, following the High Court sale order granted on 1 July 2025. The former Thomson View Condominium has been vacated and development preparation is underway.

With acquisition completed, the development is now entering the design and planning phase. Consultants and architects have been appointed. Projected Temporary Occupation Permit (TOP) remains estimated at 2032.

1 Jul 2025
2025
Milestone
High Court Grants $810M En Bloc Sale Order

The High Court of Singapore formally granted the collective sale order for Thomson View Condominium on 1 July 2025, approving the $810 million sale to the UOL · SingLand · CapitaLand consortium. At $1,178 psf per plot ratio, this is the largest approved residential en bloc transaction since Chuan Park's $890 million deal in 2023.

The High Court's approval was the final legal clearance required before the consortium could take possession of the site. The sale overcame objections from a minority of owners who had initially challenged the transaction, with the court finding the sale to be in the overall interest of the subsidiary proprietors.

Oct 2024
2024

UOL, SingLand and CapitaLand Secure Thomson View for $810M

The UOL · SingLand · CapitaLand consortium secured the Thomson View Condominium collective sale at $810 million in October 2024 with over 80% owner consent achieved — the threshold required under Singapore's Land Titles (Strata) Act for a collective sale application to proceed.

The $810 million price represents $1,178 psf per plot ratio and was the highest transacted land price for a residential en bloc site since Chuan Park in 2023. The consortium's commitment signals strong institutional conviction in the long-term value of the Bright Hill Drive location and the Upper Thomson corridor.

80% Owner Consent Threshold Achieved — Sale Application Filed

Thomson View's collective sale committee achieved the 80% owner consent threshold required to proceed with a formal en bloc sale application in November 2024. The sale committee filed the application with the Strata Titles Board (STB) following the successful consent collection exercise.

The achievement of 80% consent was the culmination of multiple years of effort by the sale committee, who had previously attempted en bloc sales in 2007 and 2018 without success. The alignment of a motivated ownership base with an institutional-grade developer consortium and a favourable market environment for large-scale D20 sites made the 2024 outcome possible.

Historical Context

The Thomson View En Bloc Story — 17 Years, 5 Attempts

Thomson Reserve stands on one of Singapore's most historically significant en bloc sites. Understanding the Thomson View history contextualises why the site commands such developer conviction in 2026.

2007
First En Bloc Attempt — Unsuccessful
Thomson View owners launched their first collective sale attempt in 2007. The reserve price could not be met in the post-GFC market environment and the attempt lapsed without a successful transaction.
2018
Further Attempts — Reserve Price Gap Persists
Subsequent attempts in 2018 and nearby years narrowed the gap between owner expectations and developer appetite, but market conditions and reserve price disagreements prevented a successful sale.
Oct 2024
$810M Deal Secured — 80%+ Owner Consent
After 17 years and multiple attempts, the Thomson View sale committee finally achieved the 80% consent threshold. The UOL · SingLand · CapitaLand consortium committed $810M — the highest price paid for a residential en bloc site since Chuan Park in 2023.
Jul 2025
High Court Sale Order Granted
The High Court of Singapore approved the $810M en bloc sale on 1 July 2025, overcoming objections from a minority of owners. The court found the sale was in the overall interest of the subsidiary proprietors.
Oct 2025
Site Acquisition Completed
The consortium completed formal acquisition on 2 October 2025. Thomson View was vacated and cleared. Development planning and design commenced. Estimated TOP: 2032.
Mar 2026
Thomson Reserve — The Development Takes Its Name
On 26 March 2026, the project was officially named Thomson Reserve — reflecting the development's position at the gateway to Upper Thomson's nature and heritage corridor. A new chapter for Bright Hill Drive begins.
